---
title: 'Declaration of Faith'
---

We at Colonial Hills Bible Chapel declare our common understanding of the Christian faith. Our goal is that we might be known for what we believe and that we might proclaim what we hold to be true, as it is written:

> “Since we have the same spirit of faith, according to what is written, ‘I believed and therefore I spoke,’ we also believe, therefore speak” (2 Corinthians 4:13).

This declaration addresses sixteen topics. It gives special emphasis to the New Testament principles of the church and to important issues of our day. Under each topic, it presents both doctrines and applications. As to the doctrines it affirms, we are in full agreement. As to the applications and supporting principles it offers, we are in strong agreement. Some in fellowship may hold personal beliefs that differ somewhat on these secondary issues. Stated here is the position of the elders and the church as a whole.
